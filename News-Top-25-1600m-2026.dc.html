Ten Of The Nation's Top 25 1600m Times Came From The ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival

ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival · April 19, 2026
Photo: Garrett Olin

When the national high school 1600m list was checked on April 19, 2026, the day after the 2026 ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival, ten of the 25 fastest times in the country had come from our boys Mile. That includes three of the top four spots, and every one of the top ten finishers in the Mile made the national top 25.

The Carnival on the national list

  • #2 nationally: Michael Ferguson (North Buncombe, NC), 4:03.35
  • #3 and #4: Riley Kiziah (Hoggard, NC) and Brooks Barbee (Christ School, NC)
  • 10 of the top 25 times, more than any other meet on the list
  • Every top-10 finisher in the Mile ran a time on the national top-25 list

Thank you to Trojan Timing for the precision behind every split.

Why it matters

Athletes from 26 states plus Washington, D.C. competed at the 2026 ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival. The Mile brought together runners from North Carolina, Florida, Wisconsin, Georgia, South Carolina and Tennessee, and all ten of the top finishers ran national top-25 times.

It was also a big weekend for Michael Ferguson, who won the 3200m on Friday night in 8:44.73 and came back to win the Mile on Saturday for a historic weekend double. Jackson Tuck also broke nine minutes in the 3200m on Friday night, running 8:57.91, and then placed seventh in the Mile on Saturday. Tuck, Bryce Cooke and Carson Dobur ran their national top-25 times in the second-fastest Mile heat, which shows the depth of the Carnival field.
