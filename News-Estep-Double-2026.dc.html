Kaitlyn Estep Doubles At The ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival, Winning The 800m And Breaking The Mile Record

ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival · April 19, 2026
Photo: Doug Hague (@hague.doug)

Kaitlyn Estep of Cardinal Gibbons (NC) had a historic weekend at the ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival. The sophomore won the 800m in 2:07.27 and then won the Mile in 4:36.56, a Carnival record, beating 2025 Nike Outdoor Nationals Mile champion Lily Alder of Timpview (UT) in a loaded field.

Taking down a loaded Mile field

Estep led the Mile at every split. She ran 1:08.8 for the first 400m and 2:19.6 at 800m. The two went through 1200m together, 3:31.3 to 3:31.4, before Estep closed her final lap in 1:05.2 to Alder’s 1:07.3 and won by 2.2 seconds, 4:36.56 to 4:38.71. Danielle Graham of IMG Academy (FL) was third in 4:40.16.

Estep’s 4:36.56 is the fastest girls Mile in Carnival history, ahead of the previous best of 4:40.82 by Carmen Alder in 2021 (1600m). Graham’s 4:40.16 also finished ahead of that mark.

The 800m win

Estep also won the 800m against a deep field that included IMG Academy’s Aurora Bertelsen Del Solar (2:08.15) and Mackenzie Winning (2:08.18). She ran a negative split, 1:04.8 for the first 400m and 1:02.3 for the second,.
