ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival Moves To Jay M. Robinson In Concord For 2027

ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival · November 23, 2026

The ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival will be held April 16–17, 2027 at Jay M. Robinson High School in Concord, North Carolina. The meet was hosted at Weddington High School from 2022 through 2026, and we are excited to bring the Mile, 800m and 3200m to a new home.

Thank you, Weddington High School

We are thankful to Weddington High School for hosting the Carnival and helping it grow over five seasons. Coach Rick Spencer and former Athletic Director Michael Hart were a big part of the meet’s success and growth, and the many Weddington volunteers they brought together helped the event run smoothly year after year. We are grateful for everything they did for the athletes, coaches and families who came through Weddington.

A MONDO Super X 720 track

Jay M. Robinson’s MONDO Track & Field Facility features a MONDO Super X 720 surface, the recommended surface of World Athletics and the Paralympics. The track is a 400-meter, eight-lane oval with 42-inch lanes and two chutes for straightaway sprint races in both directions.

Facility highlights

  • Stadium seating capacity of 3,500, fully lit
  • Warm-up and team tent areas behind the away bleachers
  • Steeplechase pit with water jump
  • Permanent restrooms and concession stands throughout the stadium
  • Field house located near the track
  • New scoreboard to display results during the meet

The facility is located at 300 Pitts School Rd SW, Concord, NC 28027, on the campus of Jay M. Robinson High School. Learn more on Explore Cabarrus.

Same partners, new location

The Carnival will keep the same partners: Trojan Timing, ASICS Running and Fleet Feet Charlotte. The location is the only thing changing, and we will keep working to enhance and elevate the event at Jay M. Robinson.

What is next

Entries for the 2027 Carnival are handled through MileSplit. The race schedule and check-in times will be posted here the week of the meet. In the meantime, see every meet record, all-time list and past champion from 2021 through 2026.
