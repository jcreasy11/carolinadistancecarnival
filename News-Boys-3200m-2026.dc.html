Ferguson Runs Down Skandera On The Final Lap As 16 Boys Break 9:00 In The 3200m

ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival · April 18, 2026
Photo: Doug Hague (@hague.doug)

On Friday night at the ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival, Michael Ferguson of North Buncombe (NC) ran down Daniel Skandera of Warrior Homeschool (AL) on the final lap to win the boys 3200m in 8:44.73. Skandera finished second in 8:46.53, and in a deep, fast race 16 boys broke 9:00.

Skandera takes it out hard

Skandera went straight to the front, running 1:03.7 for the first 400m and 2:08.6 through 800m. He hit 1600m in 4:22.3, kept the lead at every split through 2800m, and was still on the front at 7:43.9 with one lap to go.

Ferguson ran a patient race. He was eighth at the first 400m and ninth at 800m, moved up to fifth by 1200m and third at 1600m, and was second by 2000m. With a lap to go, he was 2.4 seconds behind Skandera.

Ferguson wins it on the last lap

Ferguson ran his final lap in 58.3 to Skandera’s 1:02.5, and passed him to win by 1.8 seconds. His last 800m was 2:02.9, compared with 2:08.6 for Skandera. Owen Munn of Greensboro Home School (NC) was third in 8:48.76, closing in 1:01.3 after moving from 12th place at 1200m.

A tight pack with the field in tow

Behind Skandera, the field ran as one tight pack. At the halfway point (1600m), 14 runners, including Ferguson, were bunched between 4:26.6 and 4:29.7, a spread of just 3.1 seconds, with the rest of the field right behind. That pack carried the race to the final lap, and 15 runners from the fastest heat finished under 9:00.

First 1600m vs last 1600m

The splits show how the race was won. Skandera ran the first 1600m in 4:22.3 and the last in 4:24.2, while Ferguson ran a 4:26.7 first half and a 4:18.0 second half, 8.7 seconds faster. Owen Munn also closed hard, running 4:28.3 and then 4:20.4.

Oscar Whiteley breaks 9 from the second-fastest heat

The sub-9:00 group included Oscar Whiteley of St Stephen’s Episcopal (TX), an 11th grader who won the second-fastest heat in 8:59.54. He was 21st in his heat at 800m (2:18.9), worked up through the field, and closed his final lap in 1:00.1 to get under nine minutes. He ran the first 1600m in 4:34.8 and the last in 4:24.7, 10.1 seconds faster. Jackson Tuck of Greer Middle College Charter (SC) also broke nine in 8:57.91.
