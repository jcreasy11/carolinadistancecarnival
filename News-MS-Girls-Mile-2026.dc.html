Behind Alder's World Record, Four More Middle School Girls Break 5:00 In The Mile

ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival · April 19, 2026
Photo: Doug Hague (@hague.doug)

Angelina Alder’s 4:40.98 12-year-old world record got the headlines, but it was not the only breakthrough in the middle school girls Mile at the ASICS Carolina Distance Carnival. Four more middle school girls ran under 5:00 behind her, for five sub-5:00 miles in one race.

Athena Pluchos sets a North Carolina mark

Athena Pluchos of Marvin Ridge (NC), an eighth grader, was second in 4:50.16, a North Carolina state record in the Mile. She was fifth at the first 400m and fifth at 800m, moved to third at 1200m, and closed her last 400m in 1:11.3 to finish second. Pluchos has gone from 5:06.75 in sixth grade in 2024 to 5:00.65 in 2025 and now 4:50.16.

How the race played out

Alder ran 1:08.2 for the first 400m and 2:22.1 through 800m, then closed in 2:18.8 for the second half, running 3:34.5 at 1200m and 4:40.9 at the finish. Scotlyn Szabo of Tennessee led at 800m in 2:22.0 and held on for third in 4:51.61. Molly Mocko of Illinois (4:55.00) and Jane Eller of Jenks America Track Club (OK) (4:55.43) were fourth and fifth, and all five finished within 15 seconds of each other.

Fully automatic timing (FAT) splits are provided by Trojan Timing.

A first for the Carnival

Before 2026, no middle school girl had run under 5:00 at the Carnival in our records. The previous best was Pluchos’s 5:00.65 in 2025. The 2026 race had five. Read more about Alder’s record in MileSplit’s story, and see the meet records and all-time lists for the full middle school Mile list.
