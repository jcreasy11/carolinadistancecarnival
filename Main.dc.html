Entry Standards and Seeding

Registration opens on MileSplit on January 1, 2027 and closes April 10, 2027 at 8 PM ET. The 2027 auto entry standards are coming soon and will be posted here. The seeding process stays the same.

The full Mile is run with FAT 1600m splits, but entries are based on 1600m season bests.

In registration, the system pulls your season best from the Indoor or Outdoor season. We also reference personal best, adjust marks that are drastically different, and look at marks across the 800m, 1000m, 1500m, 1600m and 3200m. Season best weighs heavier than personal best. We use the Purdy Scale to compare marks in different events, and we adjust to a mark slightly above.

Example: a 5:00.00 1600m shows 2:13.13 for the 800m and 10:52.16 for the 3200m. If that athlete entered the 800m, they would be seeded at 2:14.50.

Questions

How do I register?

Registration runs through MileSplit at $20 per athlete per event, paid by credit card. Entry standards apply. [LINK TO THE MILESPLIT REGISTRATION PAGE]

How are races seeded?

Seeds come from the MileSplit database, with this season's bests weighted most. They update Sunday night after that week's results post. Awards go to the top 3 overall in each event.

Where do teams park and set up?

Parking will be on the Jay M. Robinson HS campus, where all races and team areas are located. [TEAM AREA AND PARKING MAP COMING SOON]

Where are results and photos posted?

Trojan Timing provides fully automatic timing, with live results on MileSplit.Live. Athletes who claim their MileSplit profile get race video and photos by email.
